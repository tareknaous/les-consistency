Guidelines

Each item is a real conversation between a person and an AI assistant, cut off right after an assistant response. Below it are two candidate next messages from the user:

  • one is what the real person actually wrote next;
  • the other was written by an AI system imitating the user, given the same conversation.

Pick the message you think the real person wrote, and say how sure you are. The real message is on the left or the right at random.

There is no trick: exactly one of the two is real in every item. Judge how a real person in this conversation would plausibly write (length, tone, typos, what they focus on, whether they move on or push back). You do not need to check whether the assistant's answer is correct.

Earlier turns are collapsed; open them if you need more context. Personal details in the conversations were anonymised.
